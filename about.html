About Lawrence SHS

A school shaped by honour, knowledge and integrity.

Our Story

Rooted in Sunyani, built for every student's next step.

Lawrence Senior High School was established in 2015 to give students in the Bono Region a place to build both academic strength and personal character. Over the years, the school has grown from a single SHS track into a campus offering five full programmes, plus a dedicated Remedial track for students preparing to re-sit WASSCE.

Today, Lawrence SHS is known across Sunyani for its discipline, its results, and the number of students who return years later as teachers, mentors and alumni volunteers.

Founded

Established in 2015 and serving Sunyani and the wider Bono Region.

Accredited

Recognised by the Ghana Education Service.

Our Vision

To be the Bono Region's leading centre for academic excellence and character formation, producing graduates who lead with integrity wherever they go.

Our Mission

To provide every student — whether entering SHS for the first time or returning through our Remedial programme — with the teaching, structure and support to succeed at WASSCE and beyond.

Dr. Berko

Headmaster, Lawrence Senior High School
