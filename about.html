About Lawrence SHS

A school shaped by honour, knowledge and integrity.

Our Story

Rooted in Sunyani, built for every student's next step.

Lawrence Senior High School was founded in 2010 to give students in the Bono Region a place to build academic strength and personal character. In 2026, Lawrence College was established to extend that mission with practical skills training, youth bootcamps and WASSCE Remedial.

Today, Lawrence SHS is known across Sunyani for its discipline, its results, and the number of students who return years later as teachers, mentors and alumni volunteers.

Our beginnings

Lawrence SHS was founded in 2010; Lawrence College followed in 2026. Both serve Sunyani and the wider Bono Region.

Our Vision

To be the Bono Region's leading centre for academic excellence and character formation, producing graduates who lead with integrity wherever they go.

Our Mission

To provide every student — whether entering SHS for the first time or returning through WASSCE Remedial at Lawrence College — with the teaching, structure and support to succeed at WASSCE and beyond.
