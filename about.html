About Lawrence Schools

An institution shaped by discipline, excellence, and a God-fearing spirit.

Our Story

From Sunyani - Ghana, to the rest of the world.

Lawrence Senior High School is a private school established and operated under Ghanaian law in 2010. Located in Sunyani, it offers a values-based education with English as the medium of instruction.

In 2026, Lawrence College was established to extend the institution's work through practical skills training and youth bootcamps. Lawrence serves Sunyani and the wider Ghanaian community.

Our learning approach

Training the head, hand and heart.

At Lawrence, learning develops knowledge, practical ability and character together. Projects, collaboration and feedback help learners apply what they know, make progress and grow into responsible members of their community.

Head

Learners build academic understanding and critical thinking through a curriculum aligned with Ghana's national curriculum.

Hand

Projects and practical learning invite learners to apply ideas, work together and solve problems.

Heart

An atmosphere of love, adventure and discipline nurtures each learner's unique talents and sense of responsibility.

Guidance and regular feedback help learners reflect on their progress and next steps.

Operational principles

Faith, stewardship and community responsibility.

Lawrence is a Christian institution committed to efficient resource management and responsibility to our community. We enroll students whose educational needs can be met by the school. Our curriculum is aligned with Ghana's national curriculum, while program offerings and subject time allocations are also informed by innovation from the leadership team.
