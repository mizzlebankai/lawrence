Programs

Five SHS tracks. One Remedial path back on course.

Senior High School

Choose a track built around your strengths.

All SHS tracks run over three years and lead to the West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE).

General Arts

Literature, Government, History, Geography and Languages — for students headed toward law, journalism, education and the humanities.

General Science

Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Elective Mathematics — for students aiming at medicine, engineering and the sciences.

Business

Accounting, Costing, Business Management and Economics — for future entrepreneurs, accountants and analysts.

Home Economics

Food and Nutrition, Management in Living and Clothing & Textiles — for careers in hospitality, health and design.

Visual Arts

Graphic Design, Picture Making, Sculpture and Textiles — for students pursuing creative and design careers.

Remedial Programme

A focused, one-year track for students re-sitting WASSCE core or elective subjects, taught in small groups.

Remedial Programme

A second attempt, taken seriously.

Students who did not achieve the WASSCE grades they need — for university, nursing training, teacher training or other tertiary pathways — join our Remedial programme to focus specifically on the subjects holding them back.

  • Small class sizes, subject-focused teaching
  • Past-questions drilling and timed mock exams
  • Options to re-sit core and elective subjects independently
  • Both day and boarding options available

"The Remedial track isn't a step back — it's the most deliberate year some of our students will ever have."

Dr. Berko — Headmaster
